Core Sphere Standard Multipliers (S_k)

Water Supply (WASH):
S_water = 15 L / person / day (minimum for drinking, cooking, and hygiene)

Food & Nutrition:
S_food = 2,100 kcal / person / day (initial basic energy intake standard)

Shelter & Settlement:
S_shelter = 3.5 m² / person (covered living space requirement)

Solid Waste Management:
S_waste = 100 L / 10 households / day (~2–3 L per person daily collection volume)
